### October 9 hotfix (v2.0.1)

Fixed a progression-blocking freeze when starting the first trainer battle in Rock Tunnel. Also repaired six related data errors caused by the same patching bug. Existing Dittomon2.0 in-game saves remain compatible.

**Dittomon2.0 is here. One Pokémon. A lot more possibilities.**

The solo-mon Kanto challenge now runs on an expanded FireRed foundation, with more forms to discover and more ways to make your Dittomon your own.

### Included in 2.0

- 1,025 National Dex entries and 1,455 battle forms, including 97 standalone Mega forms. What you encounter depends on the run's pools and progression.
- PUM's persistent transformations, copied moves, ability changes, and Mewt stat customization.
- Rare Mewtated Anomalies with distinctive Ditto-eye artwork and unusual combinations.
- PokeRoll encounter variety, optional trainer randomization, and early run setup.
- Hardcore as the intended default, with a more forgiving optional mode.
- Faster PUM actions, a learned-move Relearner, and party Summary renaming.
- Refreshed opening scenes, selected classic and modern battle art, and the lilac brick interface.

### Download and start

Get **Dittomon2.0-2026-10-06.zip** below. It contains the patch, instructions, credits, and checksums. Apply `Dittomon2.0.bps` to your own clean **Pokémon FireRed USA 1.0** ROM using a BPS patcher. No ROM is included.

**September-beta players need a new save.** Keep your old ROM/save together; [that release remains available](https://github.com/Illiterally/Dittomon-Mewtation-Beta/releases/tag/public-beta-1-hotfix-1). Current expanded-rebuild battery saves are compatible; back them up and use Continue rather than an older emulator save state.

### Beta note

One intermittent stall before an early rival conversation is under investigation. Restarting mGBA and loading the ordinary save allowed that encounter to proceed in the reported case. Save normally; if it recurs, capture a save state while frozen and report the build, emulator, and preceding steps. This release does not claim a fix for that issue.

The patch was verified to reproduce the built ROM exactly. Dittomon2.0 remains a public beta, and feedback is welcome on [Discord](https://discord.gg/gNcZYYcfW).
