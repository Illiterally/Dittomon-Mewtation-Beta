## How to play

1. Download and extract **`Dittomon2.0.2-2026-10-11.zip`**.
2. Open a BPS patcher, such as [Floating IPS / Flips](https://github.com/Alcaro/Flips/releases), and apply **`Dittomon2.0.2.bps`** to your own clean **Pokémon FireRed USA 1.0** ROM.
3. Save the result as **`Dittomon2.0.2.gba`** and open it in your GBA emulator. mGBA is the main desktop testing target.

No ROM is included. Apply the patch to clean FireRed, not a previous Dittomon ROM or another hack. The required base SHA-1 is `41cb23d8dccc8ebd7c649cd8fbb58eeace6e2fdc`.

**Updating from 2.0 or 2.0.1?** Your ordinary in-game save is compatible. Close the emulator, back up your `.sav`, and copy it beside the new ROM as `Dittomon2.0.2.sav`. Start the ROM and choose **Continue**; don't load an older emulator save state.

**Starting fresh or coming from the September beta?** Choose **New Game**. September saves use a different format; keep the old ROM and save together.
