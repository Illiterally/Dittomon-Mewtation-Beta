## What's in 2.0

- **An expanded roster:** 1,025 National Dex entries and 1,455 battle forms, including 97 standalone Mega forms. Encounter pools and progression determine what you find.
- **The Dittomon loop:** persistent transformations, copied moves, ability changes, and Mewt stat customization through PUM.
- **Rare Mewtated Anomalies:** unusual wild Pokémon with distinctive Ditto-eye artwork and surprising combinations to discover.
- **PokeRoll replayability:** varied encounters and optional trainer randomization keep new runs interesting.
- **Hardcore by default:** a sharper, higher-pressure challenge, with a more forgiving optional mode and clear run settings early on.
- **Smoother tools:** faster PUM actions, a learned-move Relearner, and renaming from the party Summary screen.
- **A refreshed presentation:** classic Gen 1–3 battle art, selected artwork for later generations and forms, new opening scenes, and Dittomon's lilac brick frames.

Win with Dittomon. Scout forms, revisit areas, talk to people, and try strange ideas. The combinations are yours to figure out.

## How to patch

1. Extract `Dittomon2.0-2026-10-09.zip`.
2. Open a BPS-compatible patcher, such as [Floating IPS / Flips](https://github.com/Alcaro/Flips/releases).
3. Apply `Dittomon2.0.bps` to a **clean FireRed USA 1.0** ROM. Do not patch an older Dittomon ROM, FireRed 1.1, or another hack.
4. Save the result as `Dittomon2.0.gba`, open it in mGBA, and start a new game.

Required base SHA-1: `41cb23d8dccc8ebd7c649cd8fbb58eeace6e2fdc`.
Full base, patch, and output hashes are in `PATCH_INFO.json` inside the ZIP.

Already testing the expanded Dittomon2.0 rebuild? Your ordinary battery save is compatible with this release. Close the emulator, back up the save, and copy it beside the new ROM with the same filename stem. Use Continue rather than an older emulator save state.
